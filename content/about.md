Counting Beans is where I write the long pieces: essays that take more than an afternoon and can't be said in a blog post. If something needs time to think through properly, it ends up here.

I'm Terrance Brown, a forty-something in Cape Town. By day I run a small business; in the spaces around that I read, play the occasional game, and collect half-formed thoughts until enough of them stick together to become an essay.

There is no schedule here. Pieces get posted when they're done, which sometimes means months of quiet. If you'd rather hear from me more regularly, the shorter, looser stuff lives at [terrancebrown.me](https://terrancebrown.me).
